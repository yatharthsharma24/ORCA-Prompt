The tables bundled with ORCA are synthetic demo data. For real analysis, load observations from these sources with the ORCA ingest command:

Ocean Biodiversity Information System (OBIS, obis.org) and its Indian node IndOBIS publish species occurrence records with dataset-level citations. ORCA can query OBIS live through the obis_occurrences tool.

NOAA OISST (daily optimum interpolation SST) and NOAA Coral Reef Watch provide sea surface temperature and Degree Heating Weeks. Copernicus Marine Service distributes chlorophyll, salinity and oxygen products. INCOIS (Indian National Centre for Ocean Information Services) provides regional ocean observations and forecasts.

ICAR-CMFRI publishes annual marine fish landing estimates by state and resource. The Centre for Marine Living Resources and Ecology (CMLRE) runs fishery and biodiversity surveys in the Indian EEZ and contributes records to IndOBIS.
