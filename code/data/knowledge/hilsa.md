Hilsa shad, Tenualosa ilisha (family Dorosomatidae in the current WoRMS classification, traditionally Clupeidae), is an anadromous fish of the northern Bay of Bengal. Adults migrate from the sea into rivers such as the Hooghly and the Padma-Meghna system to spawn, mostly during the monsoon. Catches depend strongly on river discharge and freshwater flow into the estuary, and dams and barrages that block migration have reduced upstream runs.

West Bengal and Odisha land most of India's hilsa catch. Bangladesh lands the majority of the world catch and enforces seasonal bans to protect spawning and juvenile hilsa.
