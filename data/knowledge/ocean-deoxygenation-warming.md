title: Ocean warming and deoxygenation (IPCC SROCC)
source: IPCC (2019). Special Report on the Ocean and Cryosphere in a Changing Climate (SROCC), Summary for Policymakers.
url: https://www.ipcc.ch/srocc/
---
The IPCC Special Report on the Ocean and Cryosphere (2019) concluded that the global ocean has warmed unabated since 1970 and has taken up more than 90% of the excess heat in the climate system. Marine heatwaves have very likely doubled in frequency since 1982.

SROCC also reports that the open ocean has lost oxygen: a loss of 0.5 to 3.3% between 1970 and 2010 in the upper 1000 m (medium confidence). Warmer water holds less dissolved oxygen, and stronger stratification reduces mixing of oxygen into deeper layers.

The Arabian Sea and the Bay of Bengal contain some of the world's largest oxygen minimum zones at intermediate depth, which makes the northern Indian Ocean sensitive to further deoxygenation.
