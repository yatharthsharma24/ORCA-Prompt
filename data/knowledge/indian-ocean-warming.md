Roxy et al. (2014) showed that the western tropical Indian Ocean warmed by about 1.2 degC over 1901-2012, faster than other tropical ocean basins over that period, and linked part of this to changes in El Nino behaviour.

Warm years in the north Indian Ocean often follow El Nino events: 1998, 2010, 2016 and 2024 stand out in sea surface temperature records and coincide with coral bleaching in Lakshadweep, the Andaman Islands and the Gulf of Mannar.

Trends in short regional series (around 20 years) are sensitive to the start and end years and to single warm years. Report the period, the slope per decade and a significance test together.
